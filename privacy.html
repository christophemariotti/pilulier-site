The health profile is self-reported: Pilulier calculates no score or risk level and draws no diagnosis from it. It is used to adapt which readings are shown and, if you create a report, to include it for your doctor.

This information may reveal health data, yours or that of a relative you track (profiles): it only leaves your phone if you decide so (export, Android backup).

Health Connect (optional, Premium): if you connect Pilulier to Health Connect, it reads on your phone, and only for the types you allow, the blood pressure, blood sugar, weight and, if you choose them, resting heart rate, oxygen saturation and body temperature recorded by your devices (watch, blood pressure monitor, meter, scale). These readings stay on your phone, with the name of the original app and device. If you turn on the matching option, Pilulier also writes the blood pressure, blood sugar and weight you enter to Health Connect. No data read from Health Connect is sent to anyone or used for advertising. Health Connect belongs to the phone's owner: imported readings go to “Me”, and a relative's readings are never written to Health Connect. You can remove access at any time in Health Connect or in Pilulier (Connected devices → Disconnect).

4. What may leave your phone, at your initiative

  • Links and calls: Pilulier is not connected to any booking service (Doctolib, Maiia…). When you tap “Book”, “Call” or “Add to calendar”, it opens the relevant app (browser, Doctolib app, phone, calendar); nothing is sent automatically.
  • Pharmacies: the Pharmacies screen (Settings, Today, Prescriptions) opens your maps app on a “pharmacy” or “on-duty pharmacy” search, or on your pharmacy's address (directions), and dials 3237 or your pharmacy's number; Pilulier neither reads nor sends your location, the maps app uses it under its own settings.
  • Prescription sent to the pharmacy: only if you tap “Send to pharmacy”; you choose the app and the recipient.
  • Pilulier encrypted backup (optional): a copy of your data, encrypted on the phone (AES-256, key derived from your passphrase, which we never know), is written to the folder you choose (Google Drive, phone, USB drive…), on request or daily if you turn on automatic backup. The service hosting that folder only sees an unreadable file. Without the passphrase, nobody (including us) can read or restore it.
  • CSV export: you choose where the file goes (phone, cloud, sending it to your doctor).
  • Android backup: if enabled in Android settings, Google backs up app data end-to-end encrypted (only if your phone has a screen lock), under Google's own terms.
  • Purchases (optional Premium features): processed by Google Play. We only receive the purchase confirmation, never your payment details.
  • Diagnostic report (optional): only if you tap “Send by email” in Settings › Diagnostic report, after reading it in full on screen. It contains technical information (phone model and Android version, Pilulier version, notification and battery settings, reminder log for the last 14 days) and no health data: no medicine, dose, measurement or name. You choose the app used to send it; we use it only to fix the reported problem, then delete it.

6. Legal basis (GDPR)

Data is processed on your device, for your personal use, at your request (performance of the service you use). Health data is processed with your explicit consent, given by entering it and withdrawn at any time by deleting it.

7. Your rights

Your data is on your phone, so you control it directly:

  • access and portability: History › « Export full history (CSV) »;
  • rectification: edit a treatment from the Medicines tab;
  • erasure: delete a medicine (with its history), clear the app's data in Android settings, or uninstall the app.

You may also write to us at the address above and lodge a complaint with your data protection authority (in France: CNIL, www.cnil.fr).

8. Security

Data stored in the app's private storage, protected by Android's native encryption; optional fingerprint or PIN lock; screen hidden in recent apps when the lock is on; option to hide medicine names on the lock screen.

9. Children

Pilulier is intended for adults. With profiles, an adult may track a relative's treatments, including a child's; this information stays on their phone and we collect no data. The person entering a relative's data does so under their own responsibility, ideally with that person's agreement.

10. Medical disclaimer

Pilulier is a reminder tool. It does not calculate doses, gives no medical advice and does not replace the advice of a doctor or pharmacist.

11. Changes

Any change to this policy will be shown here with its date and mentioned in the app's release notes.
